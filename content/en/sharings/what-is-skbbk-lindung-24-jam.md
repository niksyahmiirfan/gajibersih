If your payslip suddenly has an extra line from PERKESO, you are not imagining it. Since June 2026, most employees in Malaysia pay into a new scheme called **SKBBK**, short for *Skim Bencana Bukan Kerja*, which PERKESO markets as **LINDUNG 24 Jam**.

## What SKBBK covers

SOCSO has always protected you against accidents at work and on your commute. SKBBK fills the gap: it covers **accidents and disasters outside working hours**, such as a road accident on the weekend or an injury at home. As the name says, the idea is round-the-clock protection, not just during office hours.

## How much it costs

SKBBK is paid **only by you**, the employee. Your employer does not add anything on top. The rate is **0.75%** of your monthly wage, worked out from the same wage bands PERKESO uses for SOCSO and EIS, and it stops rising once your salary passes the RM6,000 ceiling.

A few examples, using our calculator:

- On a RM3,000 salary, SKBBK is **RM22.15** a month.
- On RM8,000 (above the ceiling), it tops out at **RM44.65** a month.

The rate is set to rise in steps: to 1% from June 2028 and 1.25% from June 2031. We will update the calculator when those dates arrive.

## Do I have to pay it?

For **foreign workers**, SKBBK is compulsory. For **Malaysian citizens and permanent residents**, the government decided in July 2026 to enrol everyone automatically, with a window to opt out that closed on 31 August 2026. If you did not opt out, you are enrolled and the deduction will keep appearing.

Not sure which applies to you? Check your payslip for a SKBBK or LINDUNG 24 Jam line, or ask your HR or payroll team.
